Thymus Regeneration & Immune Regulation

Regulina-T™ is a biotechnology research platform focused on thymus biology and immune system regulation.

Scientific Foundation

The thymus is a central organ of the immune system where T-cells are formed and mature. These cells represent the foundation of adaptive immunity and enable the body to recognize infections and maintain immune balance.

Understanding thymus biology is a critical aspect of modern immunology and research into immune system stability.

With age the activity of the thymus gradually declines. This process is accompanied by a reduction in the production of new T-cells and a gradual weakening of immune competence.

Regulina-T™ focuses on the development of biotechnology approaches related to thymus regeneration and immune regulation.
