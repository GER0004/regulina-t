THE SCIENTIFIC CONTEXT

Thymus Biology

The thymus is a central organ of the immune system where T-cells are formed and mature.

These cells represent the foundation of adaptive immunity and allow the body to recognize infections, maintain immune balance and provide immune protection.

Read MoreShow Less

With age the activity of the thymus gradually decreases.

This process is accompanied by a reduction in the formation of new T-cells and a gradual decline in immune competence.

Understanding thymus biology is an important direction in modern immunology and regenerative medicine.

REGUCALCIN / SMP30

The scientific concept of the platform includes research into the biological role of the protein Regucalcin (SMP30).

Regucalcin participates in the regulation of cellular metabolism, calcium homeostasis and intracellular stability.

Read MoreShow Less

RGN-T1™ represents a recombinant form of Regucalcin developed for scientific research related to:

  • Thymus biology
  • T-cell development
  • Mechanisms of immune regulation

The Regulina-T™ platform investigates the role of recombinant Regucalcin within modern biotechnology research.
