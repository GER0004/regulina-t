Thymus Regeneration & Immune Regulation

Regulina-T™ is a biotechnology research platform focused on thymus biology and immune system regulation.

Scientific Foundation

The scientific concept of the platform is based on research into the biological role of the protein Regucalcin (SMP30).

Regucalcin participates in cellular metabolism regulation, calcium homeostasis and intracellular stability.

RGN-T1™ represents a recombinant form of Regucalcin designed for research related to thymus biology and immune regulation.

Thymus Biology

The thymus is a central organ of the immune system where T-cells develop and mature.

These cells form the foundation of adaptive immunity and allow the body to maintain immune balance.

With age thymus activity gradually declines which reduces the formation of new T-cells.

Biotechnology Platform

Regulina-T™ is developed as a biotechnology research platform within regenerative immunology.

The technological strategy includes recombinant protein technologies, scalable biotechnology processes and bioreactor systems.

Development follows GLP and GMP standards.
